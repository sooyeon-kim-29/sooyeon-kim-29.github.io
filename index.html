Hello! I'm Soo-Yeon 👋

I am a PhD candidate in Environmental Health at George Washington University, where I study how air pollution affects population health with Dr. Susan Anenberg. I completed my undergraduate and Master's degrees at Korea University, majoring in Environmental Health Sciences and Epidemiology.

My research aims to generate evidence that supports equitable and data-driven environmental health policy. I integrate environmental exposure models, geospatial analytics, and epidemiological methods to understand how environmental risks, especially air pollution, are distributed across time, space, and communities.

Research interests:

  • Spatial analysis & large-scale geospatial data science
  • Air pollution epidemiology
  • Health burden estimation (GBD frameworks)
  • Health impacts of climate change
  • Environmental justice and distributive inequality
