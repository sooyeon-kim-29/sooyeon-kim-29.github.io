Research

My work uses high-resolution air pollution models, geospatial data science, and epidemiological methods to understand how pollutants such as PM₂.₅, NO₂, and ozone are distributed across time, space, and communities; what health burdens they cause; and whether the policies meant to reduce them actually work.
